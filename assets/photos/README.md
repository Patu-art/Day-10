# Day 10 — Kiku Hifi real-asset brief

Instagram: https://www.instagram.com/kikuhifi/ — @kikuhifi.
Live concept: https://patu-art.github.io/Day-10/

The current website uses **original concept illustrations**, not genuine Kiku photography. Kiku has an existing professional visual identity; the demo's palette and plain-text wordmark are provisional. DO NOT invent a new logo or present Kiku's existing brand as our design. Reuse the business's current official identity once the owners provide an approved logo/style reference.

## Send these assets in the numbered order

1. `assets/photos/01-interior.webp` — **hero / priority one**. Wide real photo of Kiku's room showing timber, seating and at least one vintage speaker. Landscape 3:2 or 16:9; ideally >= 1800px wide. This is the single highest-impact replacement.
2. `assets/photos/02-coffee.webp` — actual coffee and pastries at Kiku, with bar counter or branded ceramics if possible. Portrait 4:5, >=1200px. Used for the daytime state of Coffee & Bar.
3. `assets/photos/03-evening.webp` — actual evening atmosphere: occupied bar, carefully lit cocktail/drink, vinyl/listening mood. Portrait 4:5, >=1200px. Used for the after-dark state.
4. `assets/photos/04-sound.webp` — close-up of Kiku's actual hi-fi speakers, vinyl or turntable with recognizable venue surroundings. Portrait 4:5 or landscape 3:2, >=1400px. Used for the sound section.
5. `assets/photos/05-exterior.webp` — storefront and legible venue sign/entrance, landscape or portrait >=1200px. Used for Find Us if the route/location details are approved.
6. `assets/brand/kiku-logo.png` or `.svg` — official Kiku Hifi logo/wordmark, transparent file if available, and any official palette/type reference (e.g. approved menu/brand post). Don't crop press watermarks or reconstruct the logo from unrelated brand references.

Optional: 6–10 s silent-compatible vertical video of the actual turntable or a DJ set with permission, and an approved current session poster. Do not add an invented event calendar or autoplay music.

## Integration steps

Replace hero `assets/illustrations/listening-room.svg` with item 1; daytime tab illustration with item 2; evening tab illustration with item 3; sound illustration with item 4; optionally add actual entrance photography at Visit. Use local relative paths and truthful alt text. Recheck small-screen crops, navigation and day/night switching. Keep the unofficial concept disclosure until the business adopts the site.

**Rights and accuracy:** Public Instagram/press photos are references, not automatic permission for republication. For a publicly hosted owner-facing demo, seek the venue's approval for photos and branded marks, as well as consent for identifiable people. Confirm actual customer entrance and current opening hours directly rather than copying conflicting directory records.
